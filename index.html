Message for Chacha

After the finish

Finishers walk north and exit onto Central Park West, usually 20 to 30 minutes after finishing. The Family Reunion area is West 69th to 71st Streets between Columbus Avenue and Central Park West, 11 am to 6 pm, with bag screening.

Suggested: meet on the Columbus Ave edge of the reunion area, away from the crush where runners pour in from Central Park West. For the backup, pick a landmark one block west of the screening, outside the closed zone.

    Good to know

    Never plan to cross the course
    Crossings are only at marshaled points and can be very slow. Every route here keeps you on one side, or goes under the course through the subway.
    Atlantic Av–Barclays is your underpass
    The station has entrances on both sides of Flatbush and Fourth Ave. The D, N and R platforms are on the west side; the 2, 3, 4 and Q are on the east (Atlantic Terminal) side. Walk through the station to switch sides.
    The Q is your train
    It runs from 86 St–2 Av straight to Atlantic Av–Barclays and to 57 St–7 Av by the finish. This weekend the A and C are rerouted between Brooklyn and Manhattan and some Brooklyn local stops are skipped.
    Stations near the finish
    Some stairways at 59 St–Columbus Circle, 66 St, 72 St and 81 St may be closed or exit-only, so the plan uses 57 St–7 Av.
    The flag
    Bring one you can wave without a pole, in case poles aren't allowed through grandstand screening.
    When phones jam
    Service gets spotty on First Ave and at the finish. Texts get through when calls and page loads don't, so keep the group chat as your fallback.
    Weather
    Sunny with light wind: low 50s at the start, low 60s in the afternoon.
