    Handoffs

    First Ave is the main one; Lafayette is the early backup. Hold it out at arm's length on her side, gel already opened, and let her come to you.

    Morning meet-up

    The Warwick is at 65 W 54th St, on the corner of Sixth Ave, where her parents are staying. The lobby is indoors with restrooms, and 57 St–7 Av is 3 blocks away. The N from there goes straight to Fourth Ave & 59th St, with no transfer.

    After the finish

    Finishers exit onto Central Park West 20 to 30 minutes after finishing. The Family Reunion area is West 69th to 71st Streets between Columbus Ave and Central Park West, 11 am to 6 pm, with bag screening.

    Go in order: the meeting spot first, then the backup 45 minutes after her finish, then the third option.

    Still to decide

      Good to know

      Daylight saving ends tonight
      Clocks fall back at 2 am. Phones adjust on their own; check anything set by hand.
      Never plan to cross the course
      Crossings are only at marshaled points and can be very slow. Every route here keeps us on one side or goes under the course by subway.
      Atlantic Av–Barclays is the underpass
      Entrances on both sides. The D, N and R are on the west side; the 2, 3, 4 and Q are on the east (Atlantic Terminal) side. Walk through the station to switch sides.
      The Q is our train
      86 St–2 Av to Atlantic Av–Barclays and to 57 St–7 Av by the finish. The A and C are rerouted this weekend.
      Stations near the finish
      Some stairways at 59 St–Columbus Circle, 66 St, 72 St and 81 St may be closed or exit-only.
      Google Maps doesn't know about the race
      It can't see street closures or which side of the course we need, and it may suggest the rerouted A or C. Use it to navigate, but follow our route notes for which train and which exit.
      The flag
      Bring one you can wave without a pole, in case poles aren't allowed through screening.
      When phones jam
      Service gets spotty on First Ave and at the finish. Texts get through when page loads don't.
      Weather
      Sunny, light wind: low 50s at the start, low 60s in the afternoon.
